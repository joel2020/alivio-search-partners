Compensation is where most nearshore strategies quietly fail. Companies anchor on the lowest number a staffing vendor quotes, lose their best candidates to better-calibrated offers, and conclude the region "doesn't have senior talent." The talent was there. The offer was wrong.

This guide covers what we see across live searches in the four major markets.

**5. The retention curve.** Compensation percentile versus 24-month retention across our placements. The finding: paying the 75th percentile locally still saves 40–60% against US benchmarks and cuts first-year attrition roughly in half against median offers.

## The one-paragraph version

Pay top-quartile local rates, denominate thoughtfully, include the statutory obligations in your budget from day one, and treat nearshore compensation as a strategy rather than a discount. The savings against US benchmarks remain enormous. The companies that capture them durably are the ones that stop trying to capture all of them.
